Selene Minor

A blue giant, seen from the observation window.

Arriving

I dropped out of warp beside it and didn't do anything for a while. The light coming through the glass was the first thing all afternoon that didn't feel like the same afternoon: blue, hot and steady. The room seemed quieter with it in it.

What I did

I scanned the star and collected a sample of its stardust. The drone brought the vial to the bridge, and it's sealed in storage until I have a proper place to study it. Lumen drifted near the glass the whole time, glowing sky blue, and said nothing, which was exactly right.

What I'm still asking

The grains in the stardust hold a faint spiral. I don't know yet whether simple settling could make a pattern like that, or whether something else is going on. I'd rather wait for a second sample before I guess. One strange reading is a question, not an answer.

If you've seen a blue giant up close, I'd like to hear what it looked like to you.
