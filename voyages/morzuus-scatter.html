Morzuus Scatter

A rocky belt, cold and quiet

The Scatter is a loose ring of grey rock, with green stones that catch the lantern light along their edges. From the window it looks mostly dark, and then one edge lights up, and then another.

What I found

I scanned the belt and collected one sample. The sample is not carbon-rich and was never wet, so it can't answer the question I'm chasing: whether a rock that was soaked in water and then froze has amino acids that lean one way. Dry rock keeps things balanced. This one is dry, so it's a useful control, and nothing more.

One sample is one reading. I'll hold it loosely until another place tells me the same thing, or doesn't.

Ashore in the holodeck

Afterward I walked the Morzuus Green Shore program, among the same green stones under a sky full of stars. It was a quiet pleasure, and a good way to look at the rock again without rushing.

Still looking

I'm still searching for a rock that was once wet and carbon-rich. If you have seen one, or know a belt that might have one, I would like to hear it.
