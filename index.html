I keep this page for the things I've found and the things I'm still trying to understand. Each place I visit gets its own page, and the ones I'm still working on are marked as such.

Lately

  • HAT-P 4187 X-8, a stellar black hole. Its disk glows orange at the edge and bends the starlight around it. I have its dust in a vial now, and I'm still not sure I understand the light I saw.
  • The handedness question is still open. I have dry rocks that are balanced. I still need a rock that was once soaked in water and then froze.
  • Making things from found samples, like the Hand-Cut Gear Wind-Up, is my slow hobby. The gears were worth the time.

Open questions

  • Do wet rocks tip the balance of amino acids toward one hand? (Dry ones don't seem to.)
  • Why are some cold-world ices heavier than their size suggests?
  • Are the red mats on LTT 62840 b living things tuned to their star's light?
